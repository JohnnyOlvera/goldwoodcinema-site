Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 6, 2026

This policy explains how Goldwood Cinema handles information associated with visits to goldwoodcinema.com and information that visitors choose to send through email inquiries.

1. Who We Are

Goldwood Cinema is an independent film and television production and development company founded by writer-director Johnny Olvera. Questions about this policy may be sent to johnny@goldwoodcinema.com.

2. Information We Collect

Website analytics

Goldwood Cinema uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors discover and use the website. Depending on your browser, device, settings, and location, analytics information may include:

  • Pages viewed and approximate time of visits
  • General geographic location
  • Browser, device, and operating-system information
  • Referring website or campaign information
  • Interactions such as scrolling, project-page visits, deck opens, trailer links, streaming links, IMDb links, and email-link clicks

Goldwood Cinema does not intentionally send names, email addresses, message contents, or other directly identifying inquiry information to Google Analytics.

Email inquiries

When you click an email link, your device may open your chosen email application. Goldwood Cinema receives information only if you choose to send the email. That information may include your name, email address, organization, message, and any materials you attach.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Google Analytics may use first-party cookies, including cookies commonly named _ga and _ga_<container-id>, to distinguish visitors and maintain session information. Google states that these cookies have a default expiration period of up to two years, subject to browser restrictions and visitor settings.

You may restrict or delete cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may reduce the accuracy of analytics but should not prevent you from viewing the public website.

4. How Information Is Used

Goldwood Cinema uses information to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the website
  • Understand which projects, services, and industry perspectives attract interest
  • Measure the effectiveness of professional outreach and public links
  • Respond to inquiries and evaluate potential business relationships
  • Protect the website and investigate technical or security issues

5. Service Providers and Data Processing

Google processes analytics information on Goldwood Cinema's behalf. Information sent to Google may include the URL visited, IP address, browser or device information, and interaction data. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Goldwood Cinema may also rely on hosting, domain, email, and technical service providers to operate the website and communicate with visitors.

6. Retention

Goldwood Cinema's Google Analytics property is configured for fourteen months of event-data retention. Google may retain certain aggregated reports or other information according to its own terms and settings.

Email inquiries and related business correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably useful for responding, maintaining professional records, protecting legal interests, or complying with applicable obligations.

7. Your Choices

  • You may block or delete cookies through your browser.
  • You may use Google's browser add-on designed to opt out of Google Analytics measurement.
  • You may choose not to send an email after clicking a contact link.
  • You may contact Goldwood Cinema to ask about information you previously submitted directly by email.

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide additional privacy rights. Goldwood Cinema will review reasonable requests in light of the law that applies to the request.

8. Children

This website is intended for film, television, media, and business audiences and is not directed to children under 13. Goldwood Cinema does not knowingly use the website to solicit personal information from children.

9. International Visitors

Goldwood Cinema is based in the United States. Information associated with the website may be processed in the United States or other locations where service providers operate.

10. Changes to This Policy

Goldwood Cinema may update this policy as the website, analytics configuration, service providers, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of the page will be updated when material revisions are published.
